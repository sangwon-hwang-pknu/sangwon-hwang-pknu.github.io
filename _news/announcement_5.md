AIDAN Lab has started a project under the **NRF Core Research Grants (Type A)** program on **constraint-aware offline reinforcement learning for adaptive control in dynamic integrated sensing and communication (ISAC) environments**.

The project runs from **September 2026 to August 2031**, supported by the **National Research Foundation of Korea (NRF)**.
