Prof. Sangwon Hwang will join a newly selected National Research Foundation of Korea (NRF) Basic Research Laboratory (BRL) project on AI-based adaptive underwater optical communication receivers.

Led by Prof. Wanyoung Chung at Pukyong National University, the three-year project will develop ultra-sensitive, high-speed dual-mode receiver technologies for reliable long-distance underwater optical communication.
