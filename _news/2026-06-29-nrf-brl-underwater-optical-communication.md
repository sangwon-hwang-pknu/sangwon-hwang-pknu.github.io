Prof. Sangwon Hwang is participating as a co-investigator in an NRF Basic Research Laboratory (BRL) project on AI-based adaptive underwater optical communication receivers.

The project, led by Prof. Wanyoung Chung at Pukyong National University, will develop core technologies for reliable long-distance and high-speed underwater optical communication.
