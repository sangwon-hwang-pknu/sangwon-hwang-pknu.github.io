### Drone Communication Networks

Our research focuses on the comprehensive integration of drones into next-generation wireless ecosystems, addressing two fundamental paradigms:

#### 1. Drone-aided Communication Networks
Utilizing drones as flexible, mobile infrastructure to enhance ground network performance:
- **Aerial Base Stations**: On-demand coverage for disaster recovery or hotspots.
- **Relaying Services**: Extending communication range between remote ground nodes.

#### 2. Communication for Drone Connectivity
Ensuring reliable and secure connectivity for the drones themselves as aerial terminals:
- **Control and Non-Payload Communication (CNPC)**: Guaranteed low-latency links for safe autonomous flight.
- **Payload Data Transmission**: High-throughput links for real-time mission data and video streaming.

We optimize **trajectory planning, interference management, and energy efficiency** to realize robust and scalable aerial-to-ground (A2G) and aerial-to-aerial (A2A) networks.
